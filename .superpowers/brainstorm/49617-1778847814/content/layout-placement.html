Where should TextileOS live on the site?

Three approaches to integrating your product into the VueNexa site

A

Dedicated Products Page + Nav Link

Add "Products" to the navbar. Clicking it goes to a full products.html page dedicated to TextileOS — feature list, screenshots mockups, pricing tiers, demo CTA. Homepage stays as-is with just a small "See our Products" banner linking there.

Pros

  • Full space to showcase
  • Clean homepage
  • Easy to expand for more products later

Cons

  • Visitors may miss it
  • More pages to maintain
B

Rich Homepage Section (Recommended)

Add a bold "Our Products" section directly in index.html between Services and About. TextileOS gets a full-width showcase — hero banner with product name, 3–4 key feature highlights, module grid, and dual CTAs ("Get a Demo" + "Learn More"). Nav gets a "Products" anchor link.

Pros

  • Maximum visibility
  • All content in one page
  • Drives both audiences (textile buyers + dev clients)

Cons

  • Homepage gets longer
C

Homepage Teaser + Full Product Page

A compact "Featured Product" card on the homepage (after Services) — logo, 2-line description, key stats, and a "Explore TextileOS →" button. The button leads to a dedicated textileos.html page with the full deep-dive.

Pros

  • Homepage stays lean
  • Full detail for interested visitors
  • Best of both worlds

Cons

  • Two pages to build & maintain
