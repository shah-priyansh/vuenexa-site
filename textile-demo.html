FAQ

Questions mills ask before the demo

A few common questions about rolling out loom monitoring on your shop floor.

How long does installation take?

Most mills go live within a few days. We survey your factory, set up the wireless network and server, then install a compact sensor on each loom — no long shutdowns required.

Do my machines need to stop?

No. Our WiFi-based sensors mount without rewiring your looms, so machines keep weaving while monitoring is set up. Any brief pause per machine is minimal.

Which looms are supported?

Waterjet, air jet, rapier, jacquard and circular knitting machines are all supported. If you run a mixed floor, we monitor every machine type on one dashboard.

Is training included?

Yes. We train your owners, managers and operators on the live dashboard, WhatsApp reports and mobile app so your whole team can act on downtime from day one.
