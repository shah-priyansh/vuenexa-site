Real-Time Monitoring

See every loom the moment it stops or slows

The live dashboard reads directly from each machine, so you never have to wait for a shift report to know what is happening on the floor.

Second-by-second efficiency

Every loom reports its running state, speed and output continuously — no manual rounds, no guesswork.

Instant stoppage detection

The moment a machine halts, it turns red on the dashboard with the exact time and duration of the stop.

Stoppage reason capture

Operators tag each stop — warp break, weft break, maintenance — so you know why production was lost.

Operator & shift view

Compare performance across operators, shifts and machine groups to find where efficiency leaks.

WhatsApp & Reporting Automation

Reports and alerts that reach you first

Put the right numbers in front of the right people automatically — on WhatsApp, the tool your whole team already uses every day.

Multiple WhatsApp groups

Route floor alerts to supervisors and summary reports to owners — each group gets exactly what it needs.

Instant downtime alerts

A machine stopping past your threshold triggers an automatic message, so problems are fixed in minutes.

Scheduled auto reports

Daily, per-shift and monthly production reports are generated and delivered without anyone lifting a finger.

Ready-to-share summaries

Clean production and efficiency summaries formatted for a quick glance on any phone.

Quality & Costing Analytics

Turn machine data into profit per meter

Historical quality and costing insight helps you protect margins and prove the return on every loom you run.

Quality history per loom

Track defects, breaks and rejection trends machine by machine to catch recurring problems early.

Weight & production costing

Tie production weight to cost so you always know which fabrics and machines are truly profitable.

Full traceability over time

Compare shifts, days and months to spot patterns that a single day's numbers would hide.

Data-backed decisions

Plan maintenance, staffing and orders on hard production data instead of gut feel.
